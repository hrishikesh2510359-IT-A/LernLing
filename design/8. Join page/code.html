Common Questions

What happens after I install the extension?

Lernling will automatically detect when you open study platforms like Coursera, Udemy, or edX. A tiny, cozy bamboo counter appears in the corner of your video player to count your study session minutes toward the clan streak.

Do I have to study the same subject as Aman?

Not at all! Every member of Forest Clan studies their own personalized curriculum—whether it's web development, machine learning, medical flashcards, or language learning. You share the motivation, not the syllabus.

Can I pause my streak if I take a vacation or feel sick?

Yes. Lernling has built-in Rest Days. Your companion curls up for a cozy nap and keeps your clan safe from streak penalties because real learning needs real rest.
